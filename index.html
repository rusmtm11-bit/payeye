Good questions

Before you ask.

What exactly are administrative management services? +
It's the operational back office of your business, handled by an external team: documentation and records, HR paperwork, correspondence, data management, government processes and drafting. You keep control and visibility; we do the running.
Do you work with free-zone and mainland companies? +
Both. We support mainland (DED) companies and free-zone entities across DMCC, JAFZA, DIFC, IFZA and others, and coordinate the licensing and documentation each authority requires.
How do you protect confidential information? +
Every engagement runs under a signed confidentiality agreement, with role-based access to your data and documented handling procedures. Your records remain yours, and access is limited to the people doing the work.
How does pricing work? +
We scope the work first, then quote — via a monthly retainer, a project fee, or on-demand hours. No fixed packages, and no charging for scope you don't use.
Can we start small and expand later? +
Yes. Many clients begin with a single service or a one-off project and widen the scope once the working relationship is established.
Who is our point of contact? +
A dedicated account lead who knows your business, backed by a continuity partner so work never stalls during leave or peak periods.
