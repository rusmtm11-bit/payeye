Privacy policy.

How PayEye handles the information you share with us.

Last updated: January 2026. This is a general template — have it reviewed against your actual data practices and applicable UAE law before publishing.

What we collect

When you contact us through this website, we collect the details you provide — typically your name, company, email, phone number and message. We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking on this website.

How we use it

To respond to your enquiry, provide the services you request, and communicate with you about your engagement. We do not sell your information.

How we protect it

Client information is handled under confidentiality agreements, with role-based access and documented handling procedures. Access is limited to the people who need it to do the work.

Sharing

We share information only where necessary to deliver a service you've asked for (for example, a government submission you've instructed) or where required by law.

Your choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask us to delete it, subject to any legal retention requirements.
