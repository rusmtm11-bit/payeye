Privacy policy.

How PayEye handles the information you share with us.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This notice explains how PayEye Administrative Solutions Ltd handles information you share when you contact us about our services.

Enquiries by email

The contact form prepares a draft in your email app. PayEye receives the information when you send the email. You can also email us directly.

Your email may include your name, email address, company, phone number, the service you’re interested in and the details of your enquiry. Please share only what we need to understand your request.

How we use and share your information

We use your information to reply, follow up on your enquiry and arrange the services we agree to provide. We limit access to people who need it for that work. We do not sell your personal information.

We share relevant information when needed to carry out your instructions, such as coordinating with an authorised service provider or authority, or when required by law.

Website services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its typeface from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to those services to display the site. These connections are separate from any enquiry you send to PayEye. PayEye does not use the site for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Questions and requests

Email office@payeyesolutions.com if you have questions about how we handle or retain enquiry records, or if you want to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. We will consider requests in line with applicable requirements.
