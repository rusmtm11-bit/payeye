What we handle

Comprehensive administrative management.

A full suite of administrative solutions for businesses across Dubai and the wider UAE — taken as one coordinated service, or picked to fill the exact gaps in your operation.

01 — Service

Corporate documentation & records management

The paper trail every UAE company runs on — trade licences, MOA amendments, shareholder registers, contracts and vendor agreements — indexed, version-controlled and kept audit-ready.

  • →Central, searchable filing for all corporate records
  • →Contract and licence renewal tracking with advance reminders
  • →Secure digital archiving with clear naming conventions
  • →Audit and due-diligence document packs on request

02 — Service

Office & business administration support

The day-to-day coordination that keeps an operation moving — so nothing waits on someone finding the time.

  • →Calendar, scheduling and meeting coordination
  • →Internal and external correspondence
  • →Periodic operational and status reporting
  • →Vendor and supplier administrative follow-up

03 — Service

HR administrative support

The employee paperwork that has to be right — from a new hire's first day to MOHRE-aligned records — handled quietly in the background.

  • →Onboarding and offboarding documentation
  • →Attendance, leave and timesheet records
  • →Employee file maintenance and updates
  • →MOHRE-aligned compliance documentation support

04 — Service

Government & regulatory liaison support

The processes that touch a government portal or a free-zone desk — prepared, submitted and chased so you don't have to queue.

  • →Trade licence applications and renewals (DED & free zones)
  • →Permit and approval coordination
  • →Document attestation and notarization support
  • →Liaison with DMCC, JAFZA, DIFC, IFZA and other authorities

05 — Service

Data entry & database management

Clean, structured information you can actually rely on when a decision depends on it.

  • →High-accuracy data entry and validation
  • →Database structuring, de-duplication and clean-up
  • →Ongoing maintenance and integrity checks
  • →Secure handling under access controls

06 — Service

Correspondence & communication management

A professional voice on your business email and vendor threads, with nothing left unanswered.

  • →Inbox monitoring and structured responses
  • →Client and vendor communication on your behalf
  • →Templated, on-brand business replies
  • →Follow-up tracking so threads reach closure

07 — Service

Document drafting & formatting

Reports, letters and internal policies that read like your company wrote them — because, in effect, it did.

  • →Business letters, memos and reports
  • →Internal policies and standard operating procedures
  • →Consistent corporate formatting and templates
  • →Proofreading and final polish

08 — Service

Customized administrative projects

When the need doesn't fit a box — a records migration, a licensing round, a one-off clean-up — we scope it and run it.

  • →Records digitisation and migration
  • →Backlog and clean-up projects
  • →Seasonal or overflow support
  • →Bespoke scopes designed around your operation
